About Vâlve

A listening game. Each round plays a two-minute loop from the albums on Bandcamp, and you work out its Euclidean rhythm: how many steps it divides into (n) and how many of them carry a pulse (k).

A Euclidean rhythm E(k,n) spreads k pulses as evenly as it can over n steps. E(3,8) is x..x..x. and E(5,8) is x.xx.xx. Any rotation of a pattern is the same rhythm, so the sliders only ask for k and n.

Every rhythm is a card in the deck, named after a figure from Romanian folklore and mythology. Solve the clip and the card turns over, with a few lines on who it is and a mnemonic to say along with the loop: one syllable per step, capitals where the pulses fall.
